Research tools

Free for research, teaching and personal use, with citation. Registration required.

Agent Simit · version 1.2.0

Finds and ranks hydrogen-combustion papers for your study, extracts and human-verifies data, exports open datasets and ranks kinetic mechanisms in Cantera. Mechanism differences are reported only when a paired test across papers supports them.

GTeA: Green Techno-economic Analysis · version 1.5.0

Cost, CO₂ abatement cost, price paths, and energy, water, land and ILUC indicators for biofuels, biomass, hydrothermal liquefaction and carbonisation, and biomethane from slurry and biowaste, built only on open data.

SUSAM: SUStainable Aviation Model · version 1.4.0

Cost, CORSIA carbon intensity and CO₂ abatement cost of sustainable aviation fuel pathways against fossil jet fuel; the cost of a SAF blend per flight, route or airline system; air-traffic growth to 2050; and ReFuelEU, SHT-SAF and 45Z kept apart from the raw cost.

KOR: Kinetics, Operability & Reactivity · version 1.3.1

Fuel-switch screening for burners, gas turbines, furnaces and engines: heating value, Wobbe index, fuel flow, flame temperature, burning velocity, ignition delay, NO tendency, direct CO₂, blend optimisation within limits and ideal rocket performance, with mechanism-validity labels.

User guide: what each tool needs, what it does and what you get back, with examples.

How to get access

  1. Sign in to GitHub (a free account is enough).
  2. Fill in the short form: name, institution, position, country, tool(s), intended use.
  3. Accept the invitation to the private repository; the Claude plugin is on its Releases page.

Register for access

The request is public on GitHub: do not enter an e-mail address or other private data. Registration data are used only to give access, to see who uses the tools and to follow citations.
