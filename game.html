Symposium Run

Travel through all 42 International Symposia on Combustion, from Swampscott 1928 to Brazil, across eight eras: thermochemistry, kinetics, laminar flames, droplets and sprays, turbulence, pollutants, detonation and the energy transition. Each era plays differently. Stomp the emissions, collect O₂ and air, and open the ? blocks: some hold questions on combustion and climate. Questions change every time you play.

Symposium Run is an independent educational game by Ahmet Alper Yontar. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Combustion Institute. Symposium dates and places follow the Institute's public list. Numerical answers were computed with Cantera (GRI-Mech 3.0, chemical equilibrium); global warming potentials follow IPCC AR6. City skylines are drawn freely and are not to scale. The leaderboard stores only a username, a score and a random device code; no email is collected in the game. Privacy notice (KVKK).
