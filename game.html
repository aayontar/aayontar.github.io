Symposium Run

42 levels for 42 symposia. The game is dedicated to the International Symposia on Combustion: each of its 42 levels honours one symposium, in its own host city and year, from the 1st in Swampscott (1928) to the 42nd in Brazil (2028).

Travel through all 42 International Symposia on Combustion, from Swampscott 1928 to Brazil 2028, across eight eras: thermochemistry, kinetics, laminar flames, droplets and sprays, turbulence, pollutants, detonation and the energy transition. Each era plays differently. Stomp the emissions, collect O₂ and air, and open the ? blocks: some hold questions on combustion and climate. Questions change every time you play.

Choose your hero

Why your hero changes colour. Spark is a burning flame kernel and Droplet a fuel droplet that burns as it evaporates, so both react to what they breathe. Grab O₂ and the flame burns hotter and faster: your hero glows white-yellow, speeds up and gets a 3-second shield. When the shield fades, or when you grab Air, it cools into a blue low-temperature flame, like the faint blue glow of a cool flame. Burning cooler makes little NOx and soot, so NOx and soot enemies cannot hurt you while you are blue.

Symposium Run is an independent educational game by Ahmet Alper Yontar. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Combustion Institute. Symposium dates and places follow the Institute's public list. Numerical answers were computed with Cantera (GRI-Mech 3.0, chemical equilibrium); global warming potentials follow IPCC AR6.
